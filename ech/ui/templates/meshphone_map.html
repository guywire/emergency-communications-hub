About this map

What is advertised on the AREDN mesh, placed at the node that advertises it, and whether it answers when checked from this station.

MeshPhone

Offices, trunks and people come from the MeshPhone directory and white pages served on the mesh. PBX up means the PBX answered an IAX2 poke from here. Trunk colours show latency where the PBXs report it.

Cameras and services

Each gets the one request a viewer would make. Open answered without a password, password needed asked for one; no passwords are tried. Links work from a computer on the mesh.
