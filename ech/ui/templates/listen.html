How to talk (push-to-talk)

Listening alone never transmits. Your microphone only goes out while you hold Hold to talk, or during a web phone call.

AllStar, from a desk phone:

  1. Dial 750 (or the AllStar key, then Listen to the node).
  2. Press *99 to transmit. Wait a second, then speak.
  3. Press # to stop. Transmissions time out after 3 minutes.

The 751 and 752 hub links are listen-only. To be heard on a linked node, link it with Link (transceive) above. Identify with your callsign.

AllStar, from this browser: press Talk on AllStar (the web phone calls 750), then hold Hold to talk. It sends *99 when you press and # when you let go.

M17, from this browser: press Listen on an M17 link, then hold Hold to talk. Tick Echo test first to hear yourself back from the reflector. Links marked RX only are listen-only.

M17, from a desk phone: dial 760 (761 for the second link) and use the same keys as AllStar: *99, wait for the beep, talk, then #. A low tone means the channel is busy or the link is listen-only.
